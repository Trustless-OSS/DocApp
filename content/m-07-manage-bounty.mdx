# 7. Manage a bounty

<TimeEstimate minutes={2} forRole="Maintainers" needs="A funded bounty issue + PR" />

**What you'll do:** Handle the bounty lifecycle (assign → review → pay).

---

## Assigning a contributor

Once you've labeled an issue as a bounty, contributors can comment `@Trustless-OSS /connect` (or similar) to request assignment.

To assign:

1. **In GitHub**, click **"Assignees"** on the right sidebar
2. Select the contributor
3. GitHub saves the assignment

The bot will notify the contributor they're assigned and provide a wallet connection link.

---

## Partial payouts (before merge)

If work is partial or you want to pay incrementally:

Comment on the **issue**:

<CommandBlock
  command="@Trustless-OSS /pay 50"
  description="Release 50% of the bounty now; remaining 50% waits for merge"
  where="Comment on the bounty issue"
/>

Alternatively, use these aliases (same effect):

<CommandBlock
  command="@Trustless-OSS /split 75"
  description="Release 75% of the bounty"
  where="Comment on the bounty issue"
/>

<CommandBlock
  command="@Trustless-OSS /work 60"
  description="Release 60% for work completion"
  where="Comment on the bounty issue"
/>

## Rejecting work

If the PR doesn't meet your standards:

Comment on the **issue**:

<CommandBlock
  command="@Trustless-OSS /reject"
  description="Reject the work; start the refund flow"
  where="Comment on the bounty issue"
/>

Or use these aliases:

<CommandBlock
  command="@Trustless-OSS /rejected"
  description="Same as /reject"
  where="Comment on the bounty issue"
/>

<CommandBlock
  command="@Trustless-OSS /no"
  description="Same as /reject"
  where="Comment on the bounty issue"
/>

<Callout type="warning">
  **What happens:** The bounty moves to a "disputed" state. The contributor can contest the rejection, or you can provide feedback to reopen the bounty.

## Retrying a failed payout

If a payout failed (network error, timeout, etc.):

Comment on the **issue**:

<CommandBlock
  command="@Trustless-OSS /retry"
  description="Retry the last payout transaction"
  where="Comment on the bounty issue"
/>

## Did I get assigned?

If a contributor commented asking to be assigned, you can:
- **Assign them in GitHub** (click "Assignees", select their name)
- **Or assign via bot** in a comment `@Trustless-OSS /assign @username` {/* TODO-VERIFY: exact bot command */}

## Before the PR merges

- ✅ Bounty is labeled (`rewarded` + tier)
- ✅ Money is in escrow (bot confirmed)
- ✅ Contributor is assigned
- ✅ Contributor has connected their wallet (they got the link from the bot)
