# 7. Manage a bounty


**What you'll do:** Drive a bounty through its middle stages — assignment, optional split, or rejection — before the PR merges.

---

## Assign a contributor

Assign someone on GitHub the normal way (the issue's **Assignees** field). TOSS reacts to the assignment:

- The bot links the contributor a page to connect a Stellar wallet.
- Until they connect, the bounty **parks** — no error, it just waits.
- When they connect, a worker pushes the milestone on-chain and the reward locks.

<Callout type="info">
If you assign someone while the bounty has no amount, the bot says so and the flow waits. Set an amount (tier label or `@trustless-oss <amount>`) and it resumes automatically.
</Callout>

## Split the payout (optional)

By default a merge pays the contributor the full reward. If you want to keep part of it — partial work, a shared effort — save a split **before** merging.

<CommandBlock
  command="@trustless-oss pay 70"
  description="On merge, the contributor receives 70% and the remaining 30% returns to your wallet. Percentage must be 1–99."
  where="Comment on the bounty issue or the linked PR"
/>

The bot confirms with the exact amounts:

| Recipient | Example on a 100 USDC bounty, `pay 70` |
| --- | --- |
| Contributor | 70 USDC |
| Maintainer (you) | 30 USDC |

<Callout type="info">
A split is an **intent** saved against the bounty, applied at release time. Re-comment `@trustless-oss pay <n>` anytime before merging to change it. The contributor must have a connected wallet before a split can be saved.
</Callout>

---

## Reject the work

If the work isn't acceptable, reject it. This disputes the on-chain milestone and refunds the reserved USDC to your wallet.

<CommandBlock
  command="@trustless-oss reject"
  description="Disputes the milestone, refunds escrow to your wallet, and cancels the bounty. The issue is marked cancelled."
  where="Comment on the bounty issue or the linked PR"
/>

You can also apply the `rejected` label to the issue for the same effect.

<Callout type="warning">
**Rejecting is irreversible and moves funds.** It cancels the bounty, refunds the reserved reward to your wallet via an on-chain dispute resolution, and notifies the contributor. The milestone cannot be un-rejected — you'd create a new bounty to try again.
</Callout>

---

## Force a re-check (rarely needed)

The automation parks and resumes on its own, and retries transient failures with backoff. If you ever want to nudge it:

<CommandBlock
  command="@trustless-oss retry"
  description="Re-runs the state machine now against the live rules. It cannot force a payout the automation would otherwise refuse."
  where="Comment on the bounty issue"
/>

---

## State reference

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `open` | Priced, not yet assigned. Amount still editable. |
| `assigned` | Contributor assigned. Locks once their wallet connects. |
| `paid` | Released to the contributor. Terminal. |
| `cancelled` | Rejected or closed. Funds refunded. Terminal. |
