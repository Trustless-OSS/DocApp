# 3. Deploy escrow


**What you'll do:** Create the escrow contract that holds this repo's bounty funds. You sign it in your own wallet — the platform never holds your keys.

Each repository has exactly one escrow contract. Deploy it once; every bounty in the repo draws from its balance.

---

## How signing works here

TOSS builds an **unsigned transaction** and hands it to your wallet. You review and sign it; your wallet broadcasts it to Stellar. This is why the platform is non-custodial — it never sees your private key.

---

## Deploy it

<Steps>
### Open the repository
From the dashboard, click the repo. If escrow isn't deployed yet, you'll see a **Deploy escrow** action.

### Click "Deploy escrow"
TOSS creates the unsigned deployment transaction and opens your wallet to sign.

### Approve in your wallet
Review the transaction and approve. Your wallet pays a small XLM network fee and broadcasts it.

### Wait for confirmation
Stellar confirms in a few seconds. The repo page flips to **Escrow: deployed** with a `0.00 USDC` balance.
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
Don't close the wallet popup or navigate away mid-signature. If the wallet closes before you approve, nothing is deployed — just click **Deploy escrow** again.
</Callout>

## Done when

The repo page shows **Escrow: deployed** with a USDC balance of `0.00`. The contract is live on Stellar and ready to fund.
