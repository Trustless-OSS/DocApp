# 3. Deploy escrow

<TimeEstimate minutes={2} forRole="Maintainers" needs="Stellar wallet with XLM (fee ~$0.50)" />

**What you'll do:** Create a smart contract on the Stellar blockchain to hold bounty funds for this repository.

---

## What you're doing

An **escrow** is a container on the Stellar blockchain that holds USDC bounty funds. Once deployed, only your system's commands can release the money (when PRs merge).

---

## Step 1: Click on your repository

On the dashboard, click your repository name.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-deploy-01
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/dashboard-click-repo.png
  show: Dashboard repository list with one repo highlighted or clicked
  caption: Open the repository page
  alt: Repository in dashboard list
*/}

---

## Step 2: Click "Deploy Escrow"

On the repository page, you'll see a **"Deploy Escrow"** button.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-deploy-02
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/repo-deploy-button.png
  show: Repository page with "Deploy Escrow" button (status: "Not deployed")
  caption: The Deploy Escrow button
  alt: Deploy Escrow button on repo page
*/}

---

## Step 3: Approve wallet connection

Your Stellar wallet will pop up and ask to approve the deployment.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-deploy-03
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/wallet-approve-deploy.png
  show: Wallet popup showing deployment transaction (amount: fee in XLM, destination: Trustless OSS contract)
  caption: Approve the deployment transaction
  alt: Wallet approval popup
*/}

Click **"Approve"** or **"Sign"**.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Do not refresh the page** while the wallet is open. Wait for the transaction to complete.
</Callout>

---

## Step 4: Wait for confirmation

The blockchain will process the transaction (usually 5–10 seconds). The button will change to show **"Escrow Deployed"**.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-deploy-04
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/repo-escrow-deployed.png
  show: Repository page with status changed to "Escrow Deployed" and balance showing "0.00 USDC"
  caption: Escrow is now live on the blockchain
  alt: Escrow deployed status
*/}

---

## Done

Your escrow is now deployed. The contract lives on Stellar and is ready to hold funds.

**Next:** Fund it with USDC.
