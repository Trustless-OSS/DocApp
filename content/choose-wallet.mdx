# Choose a wallet

<TimeEstimate minutes={5} forRole="Everyone" needs="A smartphone or computer" />

**What you'll do:** Pick a Stellar wallet and install it on your device.

---

## Recommended: LOBSTR

**LOBSTR** is simple, free, and made by Stellar developers. It works on iOS, Android, and web.

**Why LOBSTR:**
- Made specifically for Stellar
- Mobile app and web versions
- Clear, beginner-friendly UI
- Free (no account fees)

**Next steps:** [Create your wallet](/create-wallet) using LOBSTR.

---

## Other wallets (also work)

All of these support Stellar USDC. Pick whichever you like:

  <summary className="font-semibold cursor-pointer">Freighter (browser extension)</summary>

    • Download: freighter.app
    • Mobile: No (desktop only)
  </div>
</details>

<details>
  <summary className="font-semibold cursor-pointer">xBull (web + mobile)</summary>

<details>
  <summary className="font-semibold cursor-pointer">Albedo (web)</summary>

<details>
  <summary className="font-semibold cursor-pointer">Rabet (browser extension)</summary>
  <div className="mt-2 text-sm text-gray-600 dark:text-gray-400">

    • Download: rabet.io
    • Mobile: No
  </div>
</details>

> **What matters:** All wallets work equally well with Trustless OSS. Pick the one that fits your device. (Most people pick LOBSTR because it works on phones and desktop without extensions.)
