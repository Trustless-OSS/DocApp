# Get XLM for fees

<TimeEstimate minutes={5} forRole="Everyone" needs="Wallet created + $5–10 to buy XLM" />

**What you'll do:** Buy a small amount of XLM to cover network fees and your account's minimum balance.

---

## Why XLM?

XLM is Stellar's native currency. It powers the network (like gas on Ethereum). Every transaction—sending USDC, setting up a trustline, paying a bounty—costs a tiny amount of XLM in fees.

You don't need much. **$5 worth of XLM lasts months** for a typical user.

## Step 1: Find your account address

Open your wallet (LOBSTR). Go to **"Settings"** or **"Profile"**.

Look for your **Stellar address** (a long string starting with "G", like `GBZXN7PIRZGNMHGA7MUSC3KGDKBS63X647DQV5AXX6IMV4FVWQ6REVOLVE`).

Tap to copy it.

## Step 2: Buy XLM from an exchange

Go to a crypto exchange (Coinbase, Kraken, Uphold, or similar). Log in or create an account.

In the buy section:
1. Search for **"XLM"** or **"Stellar"**
2. Enter an amount: **$5–$10** (enough for a few months of fees)
3. Complete the purchase

You now have XLM. Next, you'll send it to your wallet.

<Callout type="info">
  **Never used a crypto exchange?** Try [Uphold](https://uphold.com) or [Coinbase](https://coinbase.com). Both are beginner-friendly. You'll need to verify your ID.
</Callout>

---

## Step 3: Send XLM to your wallet

In the exchange, find **"Send"** or **"Withdraw"**.

Paste your Stellar address (from Step 1). Confirm the amount ($5–$10).

The exchange will ask for a confirmation. Tap **"Send"** or **"Confirm"**.

## Step 4: Wait for arrival

XLM typically arrives in 1–5 minutes. Go back to LOBSTR and refresh your home screen.

You should see XLM in your balance.

## What if you run low on fees?

If you run out of XLM, just repeat this process: buy a bit more on an exchange and send it to your wallet.
