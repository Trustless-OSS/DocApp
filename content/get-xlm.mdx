# Get XLM for fees


**What you'll do:** Put a small amount of XLM in your wallet so you can pay network fees and meet Stellar's account reserve.

Every Stellar transaction — adding a trustline, connecting a wallet, deploying or funding an escrow — costs a tiny XLM fee, and an account must hold a minimum XLM reserve to exist. A few dollars of XLM covers a long run of activity. {/* TODO-VERIFY: exact minimum reserve and typical per-op fee */}

## Fund the account

<Steps>
### Copy your Stellar address
In the wallet, find your address (starts with `G…`) and copy it. {/* TODO-VERIFY: confirm where the receive address lives in the current UI */}

### Buy XLM
On an exchange that lists XLM (e.g. Kraken, Coinbase, Uphold), buy a small amount — $5–$10 is plenty to start.

### Withdraw XLM to your address
Send the XLM to the Stellar address you copied. Double-check the address before confirming.

### Wait for arrival
Stellar settles in seconds to a few minutes. Refresh your wallet to see the balance.
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
Send to the correct Stellar address. On-chain sends are irreversible — a wrong address means the funds are gone. If your exchange asks for a **memo**, a personal Stellar wallet address usually doesn't need one, but confirm in your wallet's receive screen. {/* TODO-VERIFY: memo guidance for the recommended wallet */}
</Callout>

## Running low later

If fees ever fail for lack of XLM, repeat this: buy a little more and send it to the same address.

---

## Done when

Your wallet shows an XLM balance. With USDC (trustline) and XLM (fees) in place, your wallet is ready.
