# 4. Fund escrow


**What you'll do:** Transfer USDC from your wallet into the repo's escrow. This is the balance every bounty draws from.

---

## Fund it

<Steps>
### Open the repository and click "Fund"
The escrow balance card has a **Fund** action.

### Enter an amount
Use a quick amount or type a custom one. You can fund repeatedly — amounts add to the same contract.

### Approve in your wallet
TOSS builds the unsigned transfer; your wallet signs and broadcasts it. A tiny XLM fee applies.

### Watch the balance update
Stellar confirms in seconds, and the repo's escrow balance increases.
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
Approve the wallet prompt **once**. If it seems stuck, wait a few seconds before retrying — double-approving can broadcast two transfers.
</Callout>

---

## How the balance is used

The balance is a shared pool. Creating a bounty **reserves** its reward from the free balance; the funds aren't spent until a payout releases. If a bounty is rejected or cancelled, its reservation returns to the pool. If the free balance can't cover a new bounty, the bot asks you to top up.

---

## Done when

The repo's escrow balance reflects the USDC you sent. You're ready to [set reward tiers](/m-05-reward-amounts) and create bounties.
