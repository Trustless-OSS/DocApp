# 4. Fund escrow

<TimeEstimate minutes={2} forRole="Maintainers" needs="Wallet with USDC + XLM for fees" />

**What you'll do:** Transfer USDC from your wallet to the escrow contract.

---

## Step 1: Click "Fund" on the repository page

On your repository page, click the **"Fund"** button (or look for a balance card with a "+" button).

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-fund-01
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/repo-fund-button.png
  show: Repository page with "Fund" button or "Escrow balance: 0.00 USDC" card with a fund action
  caption: Click to fund the escrow
  alt: Fund button on repo page
*/}

---

## Step 2: Choose an amount

A dialog will appear with quick options: **+$25, +$50, +$100**, or **Custom**.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-fund-02
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/fund-dialog.png
  show: Fund dialog with amount buttons (+$25, +$50, +$100, Custom) and "Continue" button
  caption: Pick an amount or enter a custom one
  alt: Fund dialog
*/}

Pick an amount or type a custom USDC amount.

Click **"Continue"**.

---

## Step 3: Review the transfer

The dialog will show:
- **Amount:** USDC you're sending (e.g., 50 USDC)
- **Destination:** Your escrow address
- **Fee:** A small fee in XLM (~$0.01)

Review, then click **"Confirm"**.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-fund-03
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/fund-review.png
  show: Fund review dialog showing amount, destination, and fee with "Confirm" button
  caption: Review the transfer details
  alt: Fund confirmation dialog
*/}

---

## Step 4: Approve in wallet

Your wallet will pop up asking to confirm the transaction.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-fund-04
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/wallet-approve-fund.png
  show: Wallet popup showing USDC transfer (amount: 50 USDC, destination: escrow, fee: XLM)
  caption: Approve the transfer in your wallet
  alt: Wallet approval for fund transfer
*/}

Click **"Approve"** or **"Sign"**.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Only approve ONCE.** Do not click multiple times. If the wallet closes, wait a few seconds and try again.
</Callout>

---

## Step 5: Wait for settlement

The USDC will transfer to the escrow (usually 3–10 seconds). The repository page will refresh and show the new balance.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-fund-05
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/repo-funded.png
  show: Repository page with escrow balance updated (e.g., "50.00 USDC" instead of 0)
  caption: Escrow is now funded with USDC
  alt: Repository with funded escrow
*/}

---

## Done

Your escrow now holds USDC. You can fund it multiple times (each time goes to the same contract).
