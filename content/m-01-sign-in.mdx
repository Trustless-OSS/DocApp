# 1. Sign in with GitHub


**What you'll do:** Sign in to the dashboard. TOSS uses your GitHub identity to know which repos and bounties are yours.

---

## Sign in

<Steps>
### Open the TOSS dashboard
Go to the TOSS app and choose **Sign in with GitHub**.

### Authorize
GitHub asks you to authorize TOSS. This grants read access to your profile and the repos you'll connect. Approve it.

### Land on the dashboard
You're redirected to the dashboard. It's empty until you connect a repository.
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Sign-in authorizes the dashboard. It's separate from installing the **GitHub App** on your repos — that happens in the next step and is what lets TOSS read issues and receive webhooks.
</Callout>

---

## Done when

You're on the dashboard, signed in as your GitHub user, with an empty Repositories list.
