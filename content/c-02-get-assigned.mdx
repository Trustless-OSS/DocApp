# 2. Get assigned


**What you'll do:** Get the maintainer to assign you, so the bounty is reserved for your work.

Only the **assigned** contributor can connect a wallet and get paid for a bounty. Assignment is how TOSS knows the reward is yours.

---

## How assignment happens

Assignment is a normal GitHub action — the maintainer sets you as the issue's **Assignee**. There's no bot command to self-assign; comment on the issue to express interest and let the maintainer assign you.

<Steps>
### Comment to express interest
Say you'd like to take it, and anything relevant about your approach. Maintainers assign people they're confident in.

### Wait for the assignment
When the maintainer assigns you, the bot comments with a link to connect your payout wallet.
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
No wallet connected yet? That's fine. The bounty **parks** and waits for you — it won't error or expire while it waits. Connect whenever you're ready and it resumes.
</Callout>

---

## Done when

You're listed as the issue's assignee and the bot has posted your wallet-connect link.
