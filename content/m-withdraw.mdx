# Withdraw funds

<TimeEstimate minutes={2} forRole="Maintainers" needs="Repository with funded escrow" />

**What you'll do:** Move unused USDC back to your wallet.

---

## When to withdraw

- You funded too much and want the money back
- You're archiving the repository and don't need bounties anymore
- You want to use the funds elsewhere

---

## Step 1: Click "Withdraw"

On your repository page, click the **"Withdraw"** button (or look for it in the escrow balance card).

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-withdraw-01
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/repo-withdraw-button.png
  show: Repository page with "Withdraw" button visible
  caption: Click to withdraw funds
  alt: Withdraw button on repo page
*/}

---

## Step 2: Choose the amount

A dialog will appear with a slider. Drag to select how much USDC to withdraw.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-withdraw-02
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/withdraw-dialog.png
  show: Withdraw dialog with amount slider and "Max" button
  caption: Select the amount or click "Max" to withdraw all
  alt: Withdraw amount dialog
*/}

Click **"Confirm"**.

---

## Step 3: Warning (if withdrawing all)

If you're withdrawing **all** funds, a warning appears:

<Callout type="warning">
  **A full withdrawal will cancel ALL active bounties and cannot be undone.**

  Any contributor currently working on a bounty will not be paid, and the money will return to your wallet.

  Are you sure?
</Callout>

Click **"Yes, withdraw everything"** to confirm, or **"Cancel"** to stop.

---

## Step 4: Approve in wallet

Your wallet will pop up to confirm the transfer.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-withdraw-03
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/wallet-approve-withdraw.png
  show: Wallet popup showing USDC transfer from escrow to maintainer's wallet
  caption: Approve the withdrawal in your wallet
  alt: Wallet approval for withdrawal
*/}

Click **"Approve"** or **"Sign"**.

---

## Step 5: Done

The USDC returns to your wallet. The repository's escrow balance decreases.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: m-withdraw-04
  type: image
  file: /media/maintainer/repo-withdrawn.png
  show: Repository page with escrow balance reduced (e.g., from 50 USDC to 0 USDC)
  caption: Funds withdrawn successfully
  alt: Repository after withdrawal
*/}

---

## Important

**Partial withdrawal:** Only withdraws that specific amount. Active bounties are unaffected.

**Full withdrawal:** Cancels all active bounties. Contributors won't be paid. Trustless OSS will refund money to their accounts (or hold it pending manual review). {/* TODO-VERIFY: exact refund flow */}
