# Withdraw unused funds


**What you'll do:** Return USDC from the repo escrow to your wallet.

---

## What withdraw does

Withdraw refunds the escrow to your maintainer wallet. On the backend this is a **refund** operation that returns the available balance and **cancels every active bounty on the repo**, releasing their reservations. The response tells you how much was refunded and how many bounties were cancelled.

<Callout type="warning">
**A withdrawal cancels all active bounties and can't be undone.** Any contributor mid-work on this repo's bounties will not be paid from those bounties, and the reserved funds return to you. Only withdraw when you intend to wind the repo's bounties down.
</Callout>

---

## Withdraw

<Steps>
### Open the repository and click "Withdraw"
It's on the escrow balance card.

### Confirm the warning
The dialog states how much will be refunded and that active bounties will be cancelled.

### Approve in your wallet
TOSS builds the refund transaction; your wallet signs and broadcasts it.

### Check the result
The balance drops and the dialog reports the refunded amount and the number of cancelled bounties.
</Steps>

## Done when

The escrow balance reflects the withdrawal and the USDC is back in your wallet. If you withdrew everything, the balance is `0.00` and you can now [remove the repository](/m-remove-repo) if you want.
