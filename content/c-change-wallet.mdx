# Change your payout wallet


**What you'll do:** Point future payouts — or a specific in-flight bounty — at a different wallet.

---

## For a specific bounty

Comment on that bounty's issue:

<CommandBlock
  command="@trustless-oss change-address"
  description="Returns a connect link for this bounty. Connecting a new wallet re-points the milestone to it."
  where="Comment on the bounty issue"
/>

`@trustless-oss wallet` does the same thing — both return your connect link.

<Callout type="info">
If you switch wallets **after** the milestone is locked, the system detects the mismatch at payout time and re-pushes the milestone to your new wallet **before** releasing. Your payout follows you; it just takes an extra moment.
</Callout>

---

## For your profile default

Your profile stores a primary payout wallet used across bounties. Update it from the dashboard:

<Steps>
### Open your profile
From the dashboard, go to your profile.

### Find "Payout wallet"
It shows your connected Stellar address, with options to copy it or change it.

### Connect a new wallet
Choose a wallet, approve the connection, and your primary payout address updates. TOSS verifies the new account has a USDC trustline.
</Steps>

## Done when

Your profile (or the specific bounty) shows the new wallet address. Future payouts go there.
