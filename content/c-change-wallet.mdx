# Change payout wallet

<TimeEstimate minutes={1} forRole="Contributors" needs="New Stellar wallet" />

**What you'll do:** Point future bounty payouts to a different wallet.

---

## Why change?

- You got a new wallet
- You want to use a different wallet for different projects
- You lost access to the old wallet

---

## Step 1: Go to your profile

On the Trustless OSS dashboard, click your avatar or name (top right).

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: c-change-wallet-01
  type: image
  file: /media/contributor/dashboard-profile.png
  show: Dashboard top right with avatar and profile link
  caption: Click your profile"
  alt: Profile link in dashboard
*/}

---

## Step 2: Find "Payout wallet"

Scroll to the **"Payout wallet"** section.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: c-change-wallet-02
  type: image
  file: /media/contributor/profile-payout-wallet.png
  show: Profile page with "Payout wallet" section showing current wallet address and "Change" button
  caption: The payout wallet section
  alt: Payout wallet in profile
*/}

---

## Step 3: Click "Change"

You'll see options:
- **Copy address:** Copy the current wallet address
- **Change:** Switch to a new wallet

Click **"Change"**.

---

## Step 4: Connect new wallet

Select your new wallet (LOBSTR, Freighter, xBull, Albedo, or Rabet).

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: c-change-wallet-03
  type: image
  file: /media/contributor/wallet-selector.png
  show: Wallet selector showing LOBSTR, Freighter, xBull, Albedo, Rabet options
  caption: Choose your new wallet
  alt: Wallet selector
*/}

Your wallet will pop up asking to approve the connection.

Click **"Approve"**.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: c-change-wallet-04
  type: image
  file: /media/contributor/wallet-approve-change.png
  show: Wallet popup confirming the new payout wallet connection
  caption: Approve the new wallet in your app
  alt: Wallet approval
*/}

---

## Done

Your payout wallet is now updated. Future bounty payouts will go to this new wallet.

---

## For a single bounty

If you want to change the wallet for **just one bounty**, comment on that specific issue:

<CommandBlock
  command="@Trustless-OSS /change-address"
  description="Get the wallet connection link for this specific bounty"
  where="Comment on that bounty issue"
/>

You'll get a link to connect a different wallet for just that bounty.
