# 1. Find a bounty

<TimeEstimate minutes={2} forRole="Contributors" needs="Trustless OSS account (GitHub login)" />

**What you'll do:** Find bounties that match your skills.

---

## Where are bounties?

Bounties live on GitHub issues labeled with `rewarded` + a tier (`low`, `medium`, `high`, or `custom`).

You don't need a special site. They're in the repositories themselves.

---

## Step 1: Go to the Trustless OSS explorer

Visit [explorer.trustless-oss.xyz](https://explorer.trustless-oss.xyz) or [bounties.trustless-oss.xyz](https://bounties.trustless-oss.xyz).

## Step 2: Filter by your interests

Use the filters to narrow down:
- **Status:** Open, In progress, Completed
- **Amount:** Low / Medium / High / Custom
- **Language:** JavaScript, Python, Rust, etc.
- **Repository:** Search by name
- **Sort by:** Newest, Highest reward, Recently updated

Click on a bounty you're interested in.

- **Reward amount** (USDC)
- **Repository name** and link

Click **"View on GitHub"** to open the issue.

## Read the issue carefully

- **Title and description:** What work is needed?
- **Labels:** `rewarded` + tier show it's a bounty
- **Bot comment:** Confirms the reward amount and escrow status
- **Comments:** Other contributors' feedback or maintainer requirements

---

## Before you request assignment

- Do you understand the work?
- Can you do it?
- Is the bounty open (not already assigned)?
