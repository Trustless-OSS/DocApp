## Step by step

### Before work starts

1. **Maintainer funds escrow.** Sends USDC from their wallet to a smart contract on Stellar. Money is now locked and can't be spent.
2. **Maintainer labels the issue** with `rewarded` + a tier (`low`, `medium`, `high`, or `custom`).
3. **Contributor sees the bounty.** Gets assigned, clicks the link to connect their wallet. Now they know money is real (it's in escrow).

### When work is done

4. **Contributor opens a PR** with `Closes #123` in the description (links to the issue).
5. **Maintainer merges the PR.** GitHub records the merge timestamp.
6. **Escrow releases USDC** to the contributor's wallet automatically. (This happens on the Stellar blockchain.)

### If something goes wrong

- **PR is rejected** (`@Trustless-OSS /reject`): Money stays in escrow. Maintainer can retry or return it.
- **PR is only partially merged** or needs more work (`@Trustless-OSS /pay 50`): Maintainer pays 50% now; remaining 50% waits.
- **Contributor never merged PR**: Money stays in escrow; maintainer can hold, retry, or withdraw.

## The escrow (what is it?)

An **escrow** is a locked container for money. It sits on the Stellar blockchain. The rules are:
- Money can only be released when the PR is merged and the contract's conditions are met.
- Maintainer can't touch the money once locked (except to withdraw all of it, which cancels bounties).
- Only the Trustless OSS system can trigger the release (automatically when it detects a merge).

This is why it's "trustless"—you don't need to trust a company to hold or release the money. The blockchain and code do it for you.
