# Trustless OSS

**Attach USDC rewards to GitHub issues and have them pay out automatically on merge — with no bounty platform holding the money.** Funds live in a per-repo escrow contract on Stellar. A merged PR that closes a labeled issue releases the reward on-chain, in seconds.

## The flow in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} icon="coins" title="Fund">
    A maintainer deploys a Stellar escrow for the repo and funds it with USDC, then labels an issue `rewarded` + a tier.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} icon="merge" title="Build">
    A contributor is assigned, connects a wallet (locking the reward on-chain), and opens a PR with `Closes #issue`.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} icon="reward" title="Release">
    The maintainer merges. A background worker verifies the rules and the escrow releases the USDC — automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why TOSS

- **Non-custodial.** You sign your own deploy and fund transactions. The platform never holds keys, and the escrow — not a company — releases funds.
- **GitHub-native.** Labels create bounties; assignment and merge drive the lifecycle; a `@trustless-oss` bot handles the edges. No separate workspace.
- **Automatic & resilient.** Bounties park when a wallet is missing, resume when it arrives, retry transient failures with backoff, and never double-pay.
- **Verifiable.** Every bounty's escrow is on-chain, so a contributor can confirm funding before starting.
