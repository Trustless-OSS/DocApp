# Trustless OSS

**Escrow-backed bounties for GitHub.** Fund open source with stablecoins (USDC), release payment when the PR merges. No escrow company, no platform fees.

## How it works in 3 steps

<Steps>
  <Step n={1} icon="git-branch" title="Fund a bounty">
    Maintainer labels a GitHub issue and funds it with USDC. The money is locked in escrow.
  </Step>
  <Step n={2} icon="git-merge" title="Do the work">
    Contributor gets assigned, connects their wallet, opens a PR with `Closes #issue`.
  </Step>
  <Step n={3} icon="zap" title="Release the reward">
    Merge the PR → USDC is released to the contributor's wallet. Takes seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why Trustless OSS?

- **No middleman.** Your money goes directly to contributors via Stellar escrow. No platform company touches it.
- **On GitHub.** Issues, PRs, labels, and commands all live in your existing workflow. No new account needed.
- **In USDC.** Fund in digital dollars. No crypto knowledge required.
- **Transparent.** Every bounty state is visible and on-chain. Contributor can verify money is locked before starting work.
- **Fast.** Payouts settle in seconds after merge.
