## Why teams use TOSS

- **Non-custodial.** You sign your own deploy and fund transactions. The platform never holds keys, and the escrow — not a company — releases funds.
- **GitHub-native.** Labels create bounties; assignment and merge drive the lifecycle; a `@trustless-oss` bot handles the edges. No separate workspace.
- **Automatic & resilient.** Bounties park when a wallet is missing, resume when it arrives, retry transient failures with backoff, and reconcile already-released chain state instead of paying twice.
- **Verifiable.** Every bounty's escrow is on-chain, so a contributor can confirm funding before starting.
