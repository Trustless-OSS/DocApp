# Add USDC (trustline)

<TimeEstimate minutes={5} forRole="Everyone" needs="Stellar wallet (LOBSTR) + XLM (see next page)" />

**What you'll do:** Add USDC to your wallet so it can receive bounty payouts.

---

## Why a trustline?

USDC lives on the Stellar blockchain. Before your wallet can hold USDC, you need to set up a **trustline**—a one-time permission that says "I allow this wallet to hold USDC."

Think of it like adding a new currency to your bank account. You do it once, and it stays.

Setting up a trustline costs a small amount of XLM (a few cents). We'll get that in the next step.
