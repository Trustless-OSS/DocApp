# Add USDC (trustline)


**What you'll do:** Add a USDC trustline so your account can receive bounty payouts.

On Stellar, an account must explicitly authorize each non-native asset it will hold. That authorization is a **trustline**. It's a one-time, on-chain operation that costs a tiny XLM fee and then persists. Without it, a USDC payout has nowhere to land.
