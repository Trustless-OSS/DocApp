# What is Trustless OSS?


**Trustless OSS (TOSS)** lets maintainers attach USDC rewards to GitHub issues and have them pay out automatically when the work merges — without a bounty platform sitting in the middle of the money.

Funds live in a per-repository escrow contract on Stellar. A merged PR that closes a labeled issue triggers an on-chain release to the contributor. You keep working in issues and PRs exactly as you do today; the automation handles everything on-chain.

## The problem it solves

Traditional bounty platforms custody your money, take a cut, and ask everyone to trust their payout process. Contributors often can't tell whether a bounty is actually funded until they've done the work. TOSS removes the custodian: the escrow contract holds the funds, and its rules — not a company — decide when they release.

## How it's different

- **GitHub-native.** Labels create bounties, assignment and merge drive the lifecycle, and a bot handles edge cases through comment commands. No separate workspace to manage work in.
- **Non-custodial.** You sign deploy and fund transactions in your own wallet. The platform never holds keys. Releases are on-chain operations gated by verified state.
- **Automatic.** A bounty advances itself: it parks when a wallet is missing, resumes when it arrives, retries transient failures with backoff, and never double-pays. Manual retry is an escape hatch, not part of the flow.
- **Verifiable.** Every bounty's state is on-chain. A contributor can confirm the escrow is funded before starting.

## Who it's for

Open source maintainers who want to fund specific issues, and contributors who want to get paid in USDC for merged work. Both sides need a Stellar wallet; everything else happens through GitHub.

## The lifecycle in one paragraph

A maintainer connects a repo (installs the GitHub App), deploys an escrow contract, and funds it with USDC. They label an issue `rewarded` + a tier (or `manual` with an amount). A contributor is assigned and connects a Stellar wallet, which locks the reward as an on-chain milestone. The contributor opens a PR that closes the issue; the maintainer merges it; the escrow releases the USDC automatically. For a detailed walkthrough with the money-safety rules, see [How payouts work](/how-money-flows).
