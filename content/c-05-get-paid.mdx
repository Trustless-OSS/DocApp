# 5. Get paid


**What you'll do:** Nothing — the payout is automatic once your PR merges. Here's how to confirm it.

---

## What happens on merge

When the maintainer merges your PR, a background worker re-checks everything against live state and releases the USDC:

1. Confirms the PR is merged, the issue closed, and you're the author.
2. Confirms the on-chain milestone matches the amount and targets your current wallet.
3. Releases the USDC to your wallet — applying a split if the maintainer saved one.
4. Comments on the issue to confirm.

Typically this is done within seconds.

## If a split was applied

If the maintainer saved `@trustless-oss pay <n>` before merging, you receive that percentage and the rest returns to them. The bot's split-saved comment showed the exact amounts ahead of time.

---

## If it doesn't arrive

The automation retries transient failures on its own, so give it a minute. Then check:

- Your PR is **merged** (not just closed), and its body has `Closes #<issue>`.
- **You** are the PR author (matches the assignee).
- Your wallet still has the **USDC trustline** and is the one you connected.

If all of that holds and it still hasn't landed, ask the maintainer to run `@trustless-oss retry`, or see the payout section of [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).

<Callout type="info">
Changed wallets after connecting? The system re-points the milestone to your current wallet before paying, so a wallet switch won't lose your payout — it just may take an extra moment.
</Callout>

---

## Done when

The bot has confirmed the release and your wallet balance reflects the USDC.
