# 5. Get paid

<TimeEstimate minutes={2} forRole="Contributors" needs="PR merged by maintainer" />

**What you'll do:** Wait for the maintainer to merge your PR. USDC releases automatically.

---

## What happens when the PR merges

1. **Maintainer merges** your PR on GitHub
2. **Trustless OSS detects** the merge (usually within 10 seconds)
3. **USDC releases** from escrow to your wallet (via Stellar blockchain)
4. **Bot confirms** the payout in a comment on the issue

---

## Check your wallet

Open your Stellar wallet (LOBSTR, Freighter, etc.) and look at your **USDC balance**.

You should see the bounty amount appear.

## Bot confirmation

The bot will comment on the issue:

```
✅ **Payout released**
Amount: 50 USDC
To: your-wallet-address
Time: 2:34 PM (3 seconds)
```

## What if the payout didn't arrive?

Check:
- ✅ PR is merged (merged status shows on GitHub)
- ✅ PR has `Closes #N` (or similar) linking the issue
- ✅ Your wallet is connected to the bounty
- ✅ Your wallet has the USDC trustline set up

If all checks pass and still no payment after 30 seconds, comment:

<CommandBlock
  command="@Trustless-OSS /retry"
  description="Ask the maintainer to retry the payout"
  where="Comment on the bounty issue or ask the maintainer"
/>

---

## Transfer your USDC

Your USDC is now in your wallet. You can:
- **Keep it** (hold for later)
- **Trade it** on an exchange for another currency
- **Withdraw** to your bank account (depends on the exchange)
