# Keep it safe

<TimeEstimate minutes={3} forRole="Everyone" />

**What you'll do:** Learn how to prevent losing money or getting hacked.

---

## Recovery phrase: the golden rules

Your recovery phrase is the master key to your wallet. Treat it like cash.

### ✅ Do

- **Write it down** on paper. Physically.
- **Store it safely.** In a drawer, safe, or with someone you trust.
- **Keep it private.** Never show it to anyone.
- **Never type it into a website.** Not even a "wallet recovery" site.
- **Keep multiple copies** (one at home, one with a trusted friend or in a safe).

### ❌ Don't

- **Don't take a screenshot.** Screenshots can be hacked.
- **Don't email it or text it.** Emails and texts are not secure.
- **Don't type it into a document** or note app (unless the device is offline and encrypted).
- **Don't give it to anyone.** Not your friend, not support staff, not "Trustless OSS" (they will never ask).
- **Don't use a password manager** unless it's specifically designed for crypto recovery phrases (e.g., Bitwarden's emergency access).

---

## The basic rule

**If someone has your recovery phrase, they own your wallet.** There's no way to get it back. Treat it like a physical key.

---

## On PINs and passwords

Your wallet's **PIN** (or password in web wallets) just locks your local account on that device. If you lose your phone, someone could unlock it and drain your wallet—unless you have your recovery phrase.

**But:** As long as you have the recovery phrase, you can recover your wallet on any device.

---

## Before you connect to Trustless OSS

Trustless OSS will ask you to connect your wallet (approve transactions) but will **never** ask for:
- Your recovery phrase
- Your PIN
- Your password
- Private keys

If anyone claiming to be Trustless OSS asks for these, it's a scam. **Do not give them.**

---

## If you lose your device

If your phone or computer breaks:
1. Install your wallet app on a new device
2. Choose "Recover Wallet"
3. Paste your recovery phrase (the 12 words)
4. Your wallet reappears with all your money

That's why the recovery phrase is so important.

---

## If you suspect you've been hacked

**Move fast:**
1. On any device, access your wallet
2. Go to Settings → Security or Account
3. Look for an option to rotate your recovery phrase or change your account
4. Move all your money to a new wallet using your recovery phrase

If someone stole your recovery phrase, they now own your wallet. You can't block them. You can only make sure they don't drain it.
