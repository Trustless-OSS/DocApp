# 3. Connect your payout wallet


**What you'll do:** Connect the Stellar wallet that will receive this bounty. Connecting locks the reward as an on-chain milestone addressed to you.

---

## Get your link

The bot posts a personal connect link when you're assigned. If you don't see it, request one:

<CommandBlock
  command="@trustless-oss wallet"
  description="The bot replies with your personal link to connect (or change) the wallet for this bounty."
  where="Comment on the bounty issue"
/>

<Callout type="warning">
**The link is tied to the assigned contributor.** If someone who isn't the assignee opens it, they get **Access denied**. This stops anyone else from redirecting your payout.
</Callout>

---

## Connect

<Steps>
### Open the link
It takes you to the TOSS connect page for this bounty.

### Choose your wallet
Pick your Stellar wallet. The page prompts it to open.

### Approve the connection
Your wallet asks you to authorize. Approve it. TOSS verifies your account can receive USDC (trustline present) before accepting.

### Get redirected back to the issue
On success, you're sent back to the GitHub issue. The milestone is now locked on-chain to your wallet, and the automation resumes.
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
TOSS checks your account has a **USDC trustline** before accepting the wallet. If it doesn't, the connection is refused — [add the trustline](/add-usdc) and try again.
</Callout>

---

## Done when

You're redirected back to the issue and the bounty has advanced past "waiting for wallet". Your reward is now locked on-chain against your address.
