# 3. Connect payout wallet

<TimeEstimate minutes={2} forRole="Contributors" needs="Stellar wallet with USDC trustline" />

**What you'll do:** Link your wallet to Trustless OSS so you can receive bounty payouts.

---

## Step 1: Get your wallet link

After you're assigned, the bot comments with a link. Click it.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: c-wallet-01
  type: image
  file: /media/contributor/bot-wallet-link.png
  show: Bot comment with "Connect wallet" link URL
  caption: Click the wallet connection link from the bot
  alt: Bot wallet link
*/}

Or request it again with:

<CommandBlock
  command="@Trustless-OSS /wallet"
  description="Get the wallet connection link"
  where="Comment on the bounty issue"
/>

---

## Step 2: Open the link

The link takes you to a page that looks like:

```
app.trustless-oss.xyz/connect?bounty=xyz
```

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: c-wallet-02
  type: image
  file: /media/contributor/wallet-connect-page.png
  show: Wallet connection page showing "Connect wallet" button and warning about only the assigned contributor having access
  caption: The wallet connection page
  alt: Wallet connection page
*/}

---

## Step 3: Click "Connect wallet"

A wallet selector appears. Choose your wallet (LOBSTR, Freighter, xBull, Albedo, or Rabet).

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: c-wallet-03
  type: image
  file: /media/contributor/wallet-selector.png
  show: Wallet selector with options: LOBSTR, Freighter, xBull, Albedo, Rabet
  caption: Select your wallet type
  alt: Wallet selector
*/}

---

## Step 4: Approve in wallet

Your wallet will pop up asking to approve the connection.

{/* MEDIA-SLOT
  id: c-wallet-04
  type: image
  file: /media/contributor/wallet-approve.png
  show: Wallet popup showing authorization request with contributor's wallet address
  caption: Approve the connection in your wallet"
  alt: Wallet approval popup
*/}

Click **"Approve"** or **"Sign"**.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Only the assigned contributor can use this link.** If anyone else tries, they'll see "Access denied". This protects your bounty from being stolen.
</Callout>

---

## Step 5: Done

The page redirects back to the GitHub issue. Your wallet is now connected.

When the PR merges, USDC will go directly to your wallet.

---

## Verify your connection

(Optional) Go to your [Profile page](https://app.trustless-oss.xyz/profile) on the dashboard and look for **"Payout wallet"**. You should see your Stellar address.

  caption: Your payout wallet is now connected
  alt: Profile with connected wallet
