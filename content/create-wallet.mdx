# Create your wallet


**What you'll do:** Create a new Stellar account in LOBSTR and store its recovery phrase safely.

A wallet is an app that holds your Stellar keys and assets (USDC, XLM). The recovery phrase it generates is the only way to restore the account — guard it like a root key.

<Callout type="warning">
If you lose both your device **and** your recovery phrase, the funds are gone — there's no reset. With the phrase, you can restore the wallet anywhere. Store the phrase offline before continuing. See [Keep it safe](/keep-wallet-safe).
</Callout>

## Done when

The wallet is created, the recovery phrase is written down and stored offline, and a PIN is set. The account is empty — next you'll add USDC and a little XLM.
