# Create your wallet

<TimeEstimate minutes={3} forRole="Everyone" needs="LOBSTR app or website" />

**What you'll do:** Install LOBSTR, create a new account, and save your recovery phrase.

---

## Before you start

A **wallet** is an app that holds your money (USDC, XLM, etc.). Think of it like a bank account, except you hold the keys and control the money directly—no bank needed.

Your **recovery phrase** is a list of 12 words that unlock your wallet. Anyone with these words can take all your money. You will write them down and keep them safe. Trustless OSS will never ask for them.

## Step 3: Write down your recovery phrase

LOBSTR will show you **12 random words**. This is your recovery phrase.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read all 12 words">
    They appear on the screen in order (usually numbered 1–12).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write them down">
    Use pen and paper. Write them in order, exactly as shown. Do not take a screenshot; do not type them into a document; do not save them in your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Store safely">
    Put the paper in a drawer, safe, or with someone you trust. Treat it like a password to your bank account—because that's what it is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Next'">

## Step 4: Create a PIN

LOBSTR asks for a PIN (4–6 digits). Use something you remember.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Do not lose your phone AND lose your recovery phrase.** If you have the phrase, you can always recover your wallet on any device. If you lose both, your money is gone forever.
</Callout>

---

## Step 5: Enable notifications (optional)

LOBSTR may ask to send you alerts. You can skip this or enable it.

---

## Done

Your wallet is now created. You have:

- ✅ Recovery phrase written down and stored safely
- ✅ PIN set
