# FAQ

## General

### What does "Trustless OSS" mean?

"Trustless" means you don't need to trust a company to hold or release the money. The Stellar blockchain enforces the rules automatically. "OSS" is open source software.

### How is Trustless OSS different from other bounty platforms?

- **GitHub-native:** Issues and PRs are your workspace. No new accounts or websites.
- **Trustless:** Money is in a smart contract, not a company's bank account. You can verify it's there.
- **Direct:** No middle-man. Your USDC goes straight to the contributor. No platform fees.

### Can I use Trustless OSS for private repositories?

Yes. The GitHub App works on private repos. Only people with access to the repo can see bounties and issues.

### Is there a fee?

No platform fee. You only pay Stellar network fees (micro-fractions of a cent per transaction).

## For Maintainers

### How much does it cost to deploy an escrow?

Just the Stellar network fee: ~$0.01 in XLM. Your wallet needs a few dollars in XLM to cover this.

### Can I edit reward amounts after creating bounties?

Yes. Change the tier amounts in settings. Future bounties use the new amounts. Existing bounties keep their original amount.

### What if I accidentally label an issue as a bounty?

Remove the `rewarded` label, and the issue will no longer be a bounty. Existing bounties (already funded) won't be affected.

### Can I pause a bounty?

Yes. Remove the `rewarded` label to pause it (the USDC stays in escrow). Re-add it to activate it again.

### What happens if a contributor abandons the bounty?

The USDC stays in escrow. You can reassign the bounty to another contributor, reject it, or withdraw the funds.

### Can I pay partial amounts before merge?

Yes. Use `@Trustless-OSS /pay 75` to pay 75% early. The remaining 25% waits for merge (or you can manually release it later).

## For Contributors

### Can I work on multiple bounties at once?

Yes. You can be assigned to multiple issues and have multiple PRs open simultaneously.

### Do I need USDC in my wallet to receive a payout?

No. Your wallet just needs the **trustline** (permission) to hold USDC. The USDC will be added to your balance when you earn it.

### What if I want to change which wallet receives the payout?

Go to your [Profile page](https://app.trustless-oss.xyz/profile) and click "Change" under "Payout wallet". Or comment `@Trustless-OSS /change-address` on a specific bounty.

### Can I claim a bounty if someone else is already assigned?

No. A bounty can only have one contributor assigned at a time. If it's already assigned, you can't claim it. Ask the maintainer if you'd like to collaborate.

### What if I need more time to complete the bounty?

Comment on the issue and ask the maintainer. They can agree to extend the deadline (or note: Trustless OSS doesn't have hard deadlines, just expectations).

### What if I submitted a PR but it wasn't merged?

The maintainer reviews it. They might ask for changes, reject it, or merge it. If rejected, you don't get paid. If merged, you do (automatically).

### Can I withdraw USDC to a bank account?

Depends on the exchange you use. USDC is on your Stellar wallet; you can trade it on Uphold, Kraken, Coinbase, or similar to withdraw to your bank. (Different exchanges have different fees and limits.)

### How long does it take to get paid after merge?

Usually 3–10 seconds after the maintainer merges the PR. You should see USDC in your wallet within 30 seconds.

## Wallet & Crypto

### Do I need to know about blockchain/crypto to use this?

No. We abstract it away. You just install a wallet, add USDC, and receive payments. The blockchain part is behind the scenes.

### Why do I need XLM?

XLM is Stellar's network currency. Every transaction (sending USDC, setting up trustlines, etc.) costs a tiny fee in XLM. You need a small amount (~$5) to cover fees for months.

### Is my USDC safe in my wallet?

Yes, as long as you keep your recovery phrase safe. Your wallet is cryptographically secured. The bigger risk is losing your recovery phrase (then you lose access forever).

### What's a "trustline"?

A permission that lets your wallet hold a specific asset. Once you add a trustline to USDC, your wallet can receive it. It's a one-time setup (no ongoing cost).

### Can I use a cold wallet?

Technically yes, but it's more complex. Most people use hot wallets (LOBSTR, Freighter, xBull, etc.) for convenience.

### What happens if I lose my recovery phrase?

You lose access to your wallet forever. The money can't be recovered. That's why we emphasize writing it down and storing it safely.
