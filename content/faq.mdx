# FAQ

## General

### What does "trustless" mean here?
No custodian holds your money. Funds sit in a per-repo escrow contract on Stellar, and its on-chain rules — not a company — decide when they release. You sign your own transactions; the platform never holds keys.

### Is there a platform fee?
No platform cut. You pay only Stellar's network fees, which are fractions of a cent per transaction, in XLM.

### Which network and asset?
Stellar **mainnet**, paid in **USDC**. These docs assume mainnet — balances are real money.

### Do private repos work?
TOSS syncs **public, non-fork** repos. Private and forked repos are skipped during sync.

## For maintainers

### What's the difference between a tier and a manual bounty?
A **tier** is a reusable `(label, amount)` pair — label an issue `rewarded` + `medium` and it uses the medium amount. **Manual** is a one-off: label `rewarded` + `manual` and set the amount with `@trustless-oss <amount>`.

### Can I rename or remove the default tiers?
Yes. `low`/`medium`/`high` are just defaults. Edit their amounts, remap them to other GitHub labels, delete them, or add new tiers. Adding a tier with a new name creates that label on GitHub. See [Set reward tiers](/m-05-reward-amounts).

### Can I change a bounty's amount after creating it?
Only while it's still editable — `open`, or `assigned` without a connected wallet. Once the milestone locks on-chain, the amount is fixed so the database and escrow can't desync.

### How do partial payouts work?
Before merging, comment `@trustless-oss pay <percentage>` (1–99). On merge, the contributor gets that percentage and the rest returns to your wallet. It's an intent you can change until merge.

### What does rejecting do to the money?
`@trustless-oss reject` (or the `rejected` label) disputes the milestone on-chain and resolves it back to your wallet, refunding the reserved reward and cancelling the bounty. It's irreversible.

### What happens to funds if I withdraw?
Withdrawal refunds the escrow balance to your wallet and **cancels every active bounty** on the repo, returning their reservations. The dashboard reports the refunded amount and the number of cancelled bounties.

### Do I need to press "release" after merging?
No. Merging a linked PR is the trigger. A background worker verifies the rules and releases automatically; `@trustless-oss retry` only exists as a manual nudge and can't force a refused payout.

## For contributors

### How do I get paid?
Get assigned, connect a Stellar wallet (locks the reward to you), open a PR with `Closes #<issue>`, and get it merged. The escrow releases to your wallet automatically.

### Do I need USDC already to receive a payout?
No — you just need the USDC **trustline** so your account can hold USDC. The reward arrives as a balance when it releases.

### Can I switch wallets after connecting?
Yes. Use `@trustless-oss change-address` (or `wallet`) for a specific bounty, or update your profile's payout wallet. If you switch after the milestone locks, the system re-points it to your new wallet before paying.

### Why do I see "Access denied" on the connect link?
The link is bound to the assigned contributor. If you're not the assignee, you can't use it. If you are, request a fresh one with `@trustless-oss wallet`.

### Can two people split a bounty?
The payout targets the single assigned contributor's wallet. The maintainer can hold back part of it with `pay <percentage>`, but TOSS pays one assignee per bounty.

### Can I work on several bounties at once?
Yes. You can be assigned to multiple issues across repos; each tracks independently.

## Wallets & crypto

### Do I need to understand blockchains to use this?
No. You install a wallet, add a USDC trustline, keep a little XLM for fees, and approve sign prompts. Everything on-chain is handled for you.

### Why do I need XLM if I'm paid in USDC?
XLM covers Stellar's network fees and the account's minimum reserve. A few dollars of XLM lasts a long time. {/* TODO-VERIFY: exact minimum reserve and typical fee figures */}

### What if I lose my recovery phrase?
There's no recovery path — whoever holds the phrase controls the wallet, and losing it means losing access. Store it offline. See [Keep it safe](/keep-wallet-safe).

### Will TOSS ever ask for my recovery phrase or keys?
Never. Any request for your recovery phrase, PIN, or private key is a scam.
